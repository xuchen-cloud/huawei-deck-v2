# Research and source discipline

## Source order

1. User-supplied or explicitly cited authoritative material.
2. Official product documentation, standards, regulation, company disclosure, and original data.
3. Primary research papers or first-party case material.
4. Reputable secondary synthesis only when primary evidence is unavailable, with the limitation recorded.

Use current sources for time-sensitive claims. Record retrieval date and version. Never use search snippets as evidence.

## Evidence-card compression

Each delegated task returns only:

- research question and supported page;
- verified fact in the Agent's own words;
- primary locator and date/version;
- direct, indirect, background, or conflicting strength;
- applicability boundary and conflicting evidence;
- proposed on-slide use;
- unanswered question or internal material needed.

Keep quotes short and only when wording itself matters. Preserve exact numbers, units, sample size, dates, and methodology.

## Sources in the deck

- Put a concise source label on the slide when the claim or data needs visible provenance.
- Put complete links and locators in a `[Sources]` block in speaker notes.
- Keep `sources.md` as the project-wide ledger keyed by page and evidence ID.
- Cite every externally sourced non-trivial claim and asset. Supplied internal files receive file and page/sheet/slide locators.

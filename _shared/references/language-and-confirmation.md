# Language and confirmation authority

Use these rules while writing intent, outline titles, evidence interpretations, and page copy. Preserve confirmed user wording unless accuracy, professionalism, or readability requires a change.

## Reporting language

- Use professional, direct, evidence-based language in titles, visible copy, and notes.
- Prefer `主体 + 动作 + 对象或范围 + 结果`. State verified fact, data, mechanism, or comparison before judgment; rhetoric is not evidence.
- Write one judgment per sentence. Move supporting information into short sentences, bullets, or a native table.
- Name concrete business objects such as product title, description, keyword, category, field length, pricing, review, delivery, interface, permission, or billing.
- Distinguish exactly: implemented, indirectly supported, inference, not covered, and not publicly documented.
- Preserve the user's original expressions, numbering, terminology, and format. Change only what harms accuracy, professionalism, or readability.
- Use notes for background, definitions, transitions, and source detail. Notes do not use host-stage directions, repeat the slide verbatim, or carry reasoning missing from the slide.

Avoid the following unless the contrast itself is evidenced or faithfully quoted:

- `不是……而是……`
- `本质上是……`
- `真正的价值在于……`
- `最值得关注的是……`
- `从 A 跃迁到 B`
- `从工具升级为基础设施`
- `赋能`、`重塑`、`颠覆`、`新范式`、`全面升级`、`生态化`、`闭环化`

Prefer concrete verbs and boundaries: `支持`、`覆盖`、`提供`、`生成`、`检查`、`评分`、`优化`、`目前`、`主要`、`尚未`、`未覆盖`、`未公开`。

Example: `AWS 将上架规范、搜索规则和质量标准内置到 AI 流程中，支持商品信息生成、合规校验、质量评分和持续优化。` Avoid: `最值得关注的不是能写标题和描述，而是平台规则已转化为 Agent 可持续执行的能力。`

## Intent information authority

Treat intent information according to its authority:

| Source | Authority |
|---|---|
| Explicitly stated in the current request or supplied material | Record as confirmed |
| Existing outline, conclusion, or decision explicitly cited by the user | Reuse with its locator |
| Personal context, historical habit, skill default, or Agent inference | Present only as a recommendation or candidate; confirmation is required before promotion |

Personal context and defaults may reduce repetitive questions or seed a recommendation. They never silently confirm audience, use, thesis, scope, stance, page count, density, evidence emphasis, or delivery format.

## Confirmation decision test

Ask for confirmation when two or more reasonable choices would change reader-visible information, evidence emphasis, conclusion boundary, or reading order. Confidence is not the test; authorship of the expressive choice is.

Group decisions that share prerequisites. Work the current grilling frontier instead of asking one question per slide. Every confirmation question includes:

1. the decision and its impact, including affected page or page group when known;
2. two to four mutually exclusive choices, or a bounded free-answer range;
3. a recommended answer and its reason;
4. the proposed fallback treatment if the user is not ready to decide.

The fallback remains a candidate, never a confirmed decision. When it blocks a gate, leave the stage pending.
